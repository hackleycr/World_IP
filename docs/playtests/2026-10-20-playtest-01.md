# Playtest 01 — Prototype 0 (2026-10-20)

Setup: one player (the project owner), Tier 0, seed 74985, about 5 minutes (303.9 s), no instructions beyond the controls.

## What the metrics say

| Metric | Result | Target ([ruleset §7](../design/02-ruleset-mvp.md#7-balance-and-measurement-d3)) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| First reaction seen | 0 s | < 5 min | Pass |
| First player-caused reaction | 4.4 s | < 5 min | Pass |
| Reactions discovered | 19 of 22 (missing: freeze, stone melt, sprout) | — | Strong |
| Materials unlocked | 7 of 8 (missing: **ice**) | — | Stuck on the last one |
| Recipes saved | 0 | ≥ 1 within 60 min | **Fail:** the recorder was too hard to use |
| Chemical energy released vs. torch energy put in | 9,298,118 vs 70,559 (~130×) | Insight beats effort (P1) | Pass: the player found leverage |

**Discovery chain timing:**

| Reaction | First happened at |
|---|---|
| Charcoal lit (`ignite_coal`) | 7 s |
| Brick (`clay_fire`) | 83 s |
| Metal (`smelt`) | 94 s |
| Glass (`sand_melt` / `glass_set`) | 144 s |

So the full charcoal → brick → metal → glass chain was found in under 2.5 minutes. Ice was never made: the only way to freeze water is to take it somewhere cold, and nothing pointed there.

## What the player said, and what changed (v0.2)

| # | Feedback | Diagnosis | Change in v0.2 | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Bit unclear how my actions are leading to consequences" | Reactions happened silently; only the journal list changed | **Labels on the world:** each reaction shows a ring and a short label where it happens ("Hot wood, no air → charcoal"). Gold for reactions the player caused, white for ones they only saw. | R8, R13 |
| 2 | "UI for temperature and location should be more obvious" | The readout was a small status line under the canvas | A **tooltip that follows the cursor** (material, temperature in large type, altitude or depth); a **temperature legend** under Thermal view | R2, R7 |
| 3 | Recorder: "very hard to use… can the game work backwards from a successful completion?" | Recording had to start *before* the player knew what would work | **Work backwards:** the game logs every stroke from the last 2 minutes. When the player makes a material, "Save how I made it as a recipe" builds one from their actions within 22 cells of the result in the preceding 90 s, with no-op repeats removed. Manual recording stays available. | R4, R12 |
| 4 | Unlocks need to be highlighted far more clearly | A one-line status message was easy to miss | A **discovery card** over the world (material, how it formed and at what temperature, a save-recipe button); **NEW** badges on palette chips until they're used; goal rows show the material's colour once found | R13, R6 |
| 5 | Couldn't figure out the last unlock (ice) | Cooling had no hint; the altitude–temperature link was invisible | **Hints the player asks for:** three per material, from a nudge to the answer. Altitude in the tooltip shows the temperature falling with height. Tested: water in a peak basin freezes in about 12 s. | R1, R7 |

The "work backwards" heuristic is spatial and time-based, not causal. It can include unrelated strokes nearby or miss a fire built further away. If playtests show that matters, tracing which cells actually influenced each reaction is the more precise next step.

## Requests for new systems (not in v0.2)

The player confirmed the direction ("definitely the right direction") and asked for the world to behave like an **earth system**. Each request maps onto new primitives, which is the long-term re-engagement path (R16):

| Request | What it needs | New primitive(s) |
|---|---|---|
| What happens if you water a desert? | Soil that holds water; sand + organic matter + water becoming soil over time; plants that need water and spread | **Soil moisture**, **organic matter** |
| How building or destroying mountains affects climate | Wind that is pushed up over terrain, cools with height, and drops rain on the windward side (a rain shadow beyond) | **Humidity** (water vapour in air), **wind** |
| How forests affect rain cycles | Plants moving soil water into the air (transpiration); losing forest dries the air downwind | Humidity + plant water use |
| Plant mix and animals, and their effects | Species with traits (water need, heat tolerance, growth rate); grazers; seed spread | **Life with traits** (ecology) |
| Where, and under what conditions, rock types form | Burial pressure compacting sediment into sandstone or shale; heat + pressure making metamorphic rock; lava cooling fast (basalt) or slow (granite) | **Pressure** (depth), **cooling rate**, **time** |

**Suggested order:**
1. **Humidity + wind (climate).** This powers three of the five requests (deserts, mountains, forests) and builds on the water cycle the prototype already has.
2. **Soil moisture and plant ecology.**
3. **Geology** (rock types from pressure, heat and cooling rate).
4. **Animals.**

The order is open for discussion.
